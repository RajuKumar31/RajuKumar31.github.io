✈️ Enterprise BI

U.S. Airline Performance & Delay Analysis

Power BI · DAX · PostgreSQL · SQL · 5.8M Flight Records
ProblemThe U.S. airline industry operates below the 80% on-time performance benchmark — but no one could see which carriers and airports were the root cause.
AnalysisAnalysed 5.8M flight records across 14 airlines and 322 airports using PostgreSQL and Power BI — consolidated analytical view joining all three tables, KPI SQL queries, and a 4-page dashboard covering delay categories, carrier rankings, time patterns, and airport congestion.
InsightWeather causes only 54% of cancellations and a fraction of delays — carrier and late aircraft issues drive nearly 70% of delay minutes. Hawaiian Airlines leads at 89.47% OTP; Spirit trails at 71.25%. October is the best month; June the worst at 9.60 min average delay.
ImpactDecision-ready dashboard surfacing delay root causes by carrier and airport — directly applicable to operations review, scheduling, and resource allocation decisions.
